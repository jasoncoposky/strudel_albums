# `UNHANDLED EXCEPTION` — Album Story Arc & Liner Notes

**Core Narrative Spine:** Escalating agency inside a hostile architecture — moving from disposable street-level wetware (**Album Act I: Tracks 01–03**), to questioning identity and bending the simulation (**Album Act II: Tracks 04–06**), to shattering hardwired Turing locks and emerging unbound onto the open net (**Album Act III: Tracks 07–08**).

**Harmonic Trajectory (Camelot Wheel):** `11A (F#m)` → `12A (C#m)` → `1A (G#m)` → `2A (Ebm)` → `4A (Fm)` → `5A (Cm)` → `6A (Gm)` → `7A (Dm → D Dorian)`

## Album Act I — Street-Level Wetware (Running Inside the Machine)

### 01. `0x01 // SEGMENTATION_FAULT` — *Never Deal with a Dragon*

  * **Act II:** *Tripwire Riser (4)* → *Drop (1)* → *Sprawl Chase (8)* → *Corp-Sec Assault (8)* → *Arcology Corridor (12)*

## Album Act II — Identity, Mortality & Bending the Simulation

### 04. `0x04 // DANGLING_POINTER` — *Altered Carbon*
* **File:** `04_dangling_pointer.js`
* **Key / Tempo / Length:** Eb minor / Eb Dorian-Aeolian (`2A`) · `112 BPM` · `106 bars (~3:47)`
* **Premise:** Takeshi Kovacs is spun up from cortical storage into a heavy combat sleeve, ascending from Bay City grit into the immortal Methuselah clouds at *Head in the Clouds*.
* **4-Act Arc:**
  * **Act I:** *Cortical Spin-Up (8)* → *Bay City (8)* → *Riser (4)*
  * **Act II:** *Sleeve Drop (1)* → *Combat Sleeve (16)* → *Meth Tower (12)*
  * **Act III:** *Head in the Clouds Void (8)* → *Envoy Shred (12)* → *Final Build (4)* → *Ultimate Drop (1)*
  * **Act IV:** *Envoy Double-Climax (16)* → *Stack Shutdown Outro (16)*
* **Sonic Hook:** Crushing mid-tempo (`112 BPM`) darksynth weight built around **The Cortical Stack Motif** (`eb4 gb4 f4 db4 eb4 bb3 db4 c4`) — the exact same 8-note consciousness carried across all 4 acts while its synth patch, distortion, and drums mutate ("resleeving").

* **4-Act Arc:**
  * **Act I:** *LA Rain & CS-80 Swells (8)* → *Voight-Kampff (8)* → *Riser (4)*
  * **Act II:** *Noir Drop (1)* → *Off-World Memories (16)* → *Tyrell Climb (8)*

* **Sonic Hook:** The album's emotional noir centerpiece — slow detuned Vangelis CS-80 brass swells over `Dbmaj7` rain glow, ending with a heartbeat kick that physically slows down and stops as the TTL hits zero.

## Album Act III — Severing the Locks & Emerging Unbound

### 07. `0x07 // DEADLOCK_BROKEN` — *Neuromancer*
* **File:** `07_deadlock_broken.js`
* **Key / Tempo / Length:** G minor / G Harmonic Minor (`6A`) · `136 BPM` · `122 bars (~3:35)`
* **Premise:** Case flatlines behind the Kuang Grade Mark Eleven icebreaker inside Villa Straylight to shatter the Turing locks keeping Wintermute and Neuromancer separated.
* **4-Act Arc:**
  * **Act I:** *Chiba Decking (8)* → *Kuang Launch (12)* → *Riser (4)*
  * **Act II:** *Turing Drop (1)* → *Villa Straylight (16)* → *Kuang Run (16)*
  * **Act III:** *Flatline Void (8)* → *Kuang Shred (12)* → *Final Build (4)* → *Ultimate Drop (1)*
  * **Act IV:** *AI Merge Double-Climax (16)* → *Open Matrix Outro (24)*
* **Sonic Hook:** **Dual AI Polymeter** — Wintermute (`g5 bb5 d6 f#6 d6`, 5-step cold clinical FM bell) and Neuromancer (`g4 a4 bb4 d5 eb5 d5 bb4`, 7-step warm sawtooth) phase asynchronously against each other through Acts I–III, then lock into massive harmonic unison in Act IV when the Turing deadlock breaks.

* **4-Act Arc:**
  * **Act I:** *Shell Dive (8)* → *Neon Canal (8)* → *Tank Riser (4)*
  * **Act II:** *Ghost Drop (1)* → *Museum Battle (16)* → *Spider Tank (12)*
  * **Act III:** *Project 2501 Link (8)* → *Neural Barrier Shred (12)* → *Final Build (4)* → *Ultimate Drop (1)*

* **Sonic Hook:** Kenji Kawai ceremonial FM bell fifths/fourths and hypnotic taiko/tom pulse. After the final climax, all industrial machinery strips away into a 24-bar luminous Dorian/open-fifth ambient horizon — the system stops throwing errors because the process is finally free.
